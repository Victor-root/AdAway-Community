    The fork

    Why this exists

    AdAway is a great project, but its updates had slowed down and some useful fixes were left waiting. This fork keeps them alive in a build you can actually install.

    Version 7

    Rebuilt from scratch

    AdAway relied on very old code and tools. Patching them any longer was not an option if the app was to stay stable, fast, secure and easy to maintain, so everything was rebuilt on modern foundations. It was a big job, but an unavoidable one.

    For you, nothing changes: same screens, same settings, and your lists are kept. Behind the scenes, everything is new.

    This is not a hostile fork: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or signed by the official AdAway maintainers.

    How it works

    Blocking at the source

    Ads and trackers must look up a domain name before they can load. AdAway answers those lookups with nothing, so the request never leaves your device, in every app.

    No root needed

    VPN mode

    A local VPN on your device inspects DNS requests only. No account, and nothing is sent to a remote server.

    Root required

    Root mode

    On a rooted device, the blocklist is written straight into the system hosts file. The VPN slot stays free.

    Features

    What you get

    Stable VPN mode

    No restart loops, and the app, the notification and the Quick Settings tile always agree. The VPN comes back on its own if your phone kills it.

    Android TV

    A full interface built for a remote, on TVs and TV boxes.

    Live DNS log

    See what each app asks for, then block, allow or redirect it in one tap.

    Your own lists

    Your own blocked, allowed and redirected hosts, and per-app VPN exclusions.
